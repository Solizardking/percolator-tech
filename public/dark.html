Part I · The Engine

Percolator is an on-chain perpetuals DEX built around a single slab account. Config, risk engine and up to 4,096 trader records sit in one 1,755,520-byte layout, so every trade, liquidation and funding accrual is a mutation of that one account.

Bounty4 (bounty_stoxx50_sol_20x_hybrid) is a 20× perp on the STOXX 50 priced in SOL. Its insurance fund was seeded with 5 SOL, and the bounty is won by making that balance go down through public instructions.

01 · Pricing

The three-leg oracle

Solana runs 24/7; Euronext does not. Pricing STOXX 50 in SOL takes three Pyth PriceUpdateV2 feeds with different schedules, combined in e6 integer math, then inverted.

raw  = Leg1 × Leg2 / Leg3  // STOXX50/EUR × EUR/USD ÷ SOL/USD
mark = 10¹² / raw_e6          // invert = 1 in config

02 · Schedules

The clock problem

Leg 1 stops publishing when Euronext closes at 17:30 Paris time. In November that is 16:30 UTC; in summer it is 15:30 UTC. Once Leg 1 is older than 600 s the wrapper switches to HYBRID_AFTER_HOURS: EWMA mark and a dynamic fee.

03 · Stale-oracle pricing

The EWMA mark keeps the market alive

decay    = 2^(−dt / halflife)  // halflife = 6,480,000 slots ≈ 30 days
ewma_new = ewma_prev × decay + target × (1 − decay)

With a 30-day half-life, an overnight gap of roughly 54,000 slots (6 hours at 400 ms) moves the EWMA by well under one percent of the gap to its target. The mark stays stable after hours and re-anchors once the live composite returns.

04 · Defense

The hybrid fee makes manipulation lose money

fee_bps = min( base_bps + |ewma_now − ewma_ref| × 10⁴ / ewma_ref , 10,000 )
base_bps = 1

Anyone who drags the after-hours mark by X bps can earn at most X bps of notional from it, and pays 1 + X bps to do so. The best case is a loss of the base fee.

Part II · Design Proposal
09 · Motivation

Dark Percolator

The same risk engine, executed inside a MagicBlock Private Ephemeral Rollup on Intel TDX. Positions, intents and liquidation prices are readable only by the accounts the program lists. Collateral, insurance and aggregate open interest still settle on Solana where anyone can audit them.

Status: design, devnet-first Nothing deployed or funded Our composition, not a MagicBlock product

What a public slab leaks

Every Percolator account record is world-readable. Anyone can decode the slab, sort accounts by leverage exactly as the crank does, and compute each trader’s liquidation price. That invites stop-hunting near thin after-hours books, copy-trading of size, and front-running of intent.

A Private Ephemeral Rollup changes who can read state, not what the program does. Delegated accounts execute in a TEE validator at ER speed; the permission program decides which wallets see logs, balances and messages; and state commits back to Solana on our schedule.

11 · The key change

Break the slab apart

Permissions in a PER are per account. If we delegate the 1.75 MB slab as-is, its member list is all-or-nothing: every listed wallet sees every trader. So Dark Percolator splits state:

Market account (one)

Vault, insurance fund, params, EWMA, aggregate long and short OI, funding index. Delegated with an empty member list while trading runs, and its aggregates are published on every commit so insurance solvency stays auditable.

Position PDA (one per trader)

Seeds ["pos", market, owner]. Holds size, entry, margin and PnL. Its EphemeralPermission lists the owner with TX_LOGS | TX_BALANCES | TX_MESSAGE plus an optional auditor key. Rent for the permission is pre-funded at open.

The crank no longer sorts accounts off-chain by public leverage. It runs inside the TEE over the PDAs the market tracks, so nobody outside can build the liquidation queue.

13 · Liquidity

Dark pools for Phoenix and Solana perps

Traders submit sealed intents into the PER. Each batch crosses buys against sells at the oracle mark, pro rata on the heavier side. Only the net residual leaves the rollup, as a hedge order on Phoenix fired by a Magic Action after the commit lands. Matched flow never touches a public book.

One trader, one round trip

Program sketch

Shape only, against ephemeral-rollups-sdk v0.14+. Not compiled or audited.

#[ephemeral]
#[program]
pub mod dark_percolator {
    /// base: create position PDA, take collateral, pre-fund permission rent
    pub fn open_position(ctx: Context<Open>, collateral: u64) -> Result<()>;

    /// base: delegate PDA to the TEE validator
    pub fn delegate_position(ctx: Context<DelegatePos>) -> Result<()>;

    /// ER: PDA-signed CreateEphemeralPermissionCpi, then make private
    pub fn seal(ctx: Context<PermCtx>, auditor: Option<Pubkey>) -> Result<()> {
        let mut members = vec![Member {
            flags: TX_LOGS_FLAG | TX_BALANCES_FLAG | TX_MESSAGE_FLAG,
            pubkey: ctx.accounts.position.owner,
        }];
        if let Some(a) = auditor {
            members.push(Member { flags: TX_BALANCES_FLAG, pubkey: a });
        }
        UpdateEphemeralPermissionCpi { /* … */
            args: EphemeralMembersArgs { is_private: true, members },
        }.invoke_signed(&[&pos_seeds])?;
        Ok(())
    }

    /// ER: sealed intent, readable only by owner + auditor
    pub fn submit_intent(ctx: Context<Intent>, side: Side, size: u64, limit_e6: u64) -> Result<()>;

    /// ER: cross the batch at the composite/EWMA mark, pro rata
    pub fn cross_batch(ctx: Context<Cross>) -> Result<()>;

    /// ER: funding, liquidation, EWMA — same engine as Percolator
    pub fn crank(ctx: Context<Crank>) -> Result<()>;

    /// ER: commit aggregates, then hedge residual on Phoenix
    pub fn commit_and_hedge(ctx: Context<CommitHedge>) -> Result<()> {
        MagicIntentBundleBuilder::new(payer, magic_context, magic_program)
            .commit(&[ctx.accounts.market.to_account_info()])
            .add_post_commit_actions([hedge_residual_action])
            .build_and_invoke()?;
        Ok(())
    }

    /// base: called by the post-commit action; MUST check the escrow signer
    pub fn hedge_residual(ctx: Context<Hedge>) -> Result<()>;
}
15 · Constraints

What this does not solve

ConstraintReality
Hardware trustPrivacy rests on Intel TDX and on MagicBlock running the validator honestly. It is a vendor trust assumption, not a cryptographic proof.
Hosted and gatedA PER cannot be self-hosted, and mainnet ER endpoints are team-gated. Devnet devnet-tee.magicblock.app is the only open path today.
Compliance at ingressMagicBlock blocks restricted jurisdictions by IP and screens flows with Range. Dark Percolator is private, not anonymous, and some users will be refused.
Commit costsAn account without a fee payer stops after 10 commits; with one, live commit fees start at commit 26. Commit cadence sets how fresh public aggregates are.
Oracle inside the ERThe three-leg composite and its 600 s rule must hold on whatever price path the TEE reads, or the hybrid fee loses its anchor.
Same engine bugsFindings D, B, N and I move with the engine. Hiding state makes them harder to observe, not fixed.

Perps at 20× are high risk, dark or not. Nothing here is a recommendation to trade.

16 · Plan

Build plan

D0

Run MagicBlock’s private-counter example end to end on devnet TEE: delegate, permission, auth token, private read, undelegate.

D1

Split the slab: market account plus per-trader position PDAs on devnet with test collateral. Port the engine unchanged and diff outputs against public Percolator.

D2

Sealed intents and batch crossing at the composite mark. Publish aggregate OI and insurance on each commit.

D3

Residual hedge to Phoenix devnet through a post-commit Magic Action with an escrow-signer check.

D4

External audit, MagicBlock mainnet access, then a separate go-ahead and funding decision. Independent of Percolator Route A.
